ENGINEERING STAMP / SEAL SERVICE

Engineering stamp/seal support

For this state, TOMZY can intake the project, review the supplied drawings/calculations and coordinate the appropriate professional-engineering review and sealing pathway when the project is eligible.

01 · Send the project
Upload drawings, calculations, location, scope and any jurisdiction comments.
02 · Technical review
The applicable professional scope and state requirements are checked before sealing.
03 · Licensed professional
Where a seal is required, the final professional seal must come from the appropriately licensed engineer responsible for the work.
04 · Deliverable
The reviewed/sealed documents are returned according to the agreed project scope.
A stamp is not simply an image added to a drawing. Final engineering sealing depends on professional licensure, responsible charge, discipline, project scope and the requirements of the applicable jurisdiction.
